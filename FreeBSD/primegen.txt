NAME
     primegaps - print large prime gaps

SYNTAX
     primegaps

DESCRIPTION
     primegaps searches for large gaps in the sequence of primes.  The gaps
     between 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, and so on are 1, 2, 2, 4, and so on.

     When primegaps sees a new largest gap n, from p-n to p, it prints a line
     p n r where r is the approximate ratio between the log of n and the log
     of the log of p.

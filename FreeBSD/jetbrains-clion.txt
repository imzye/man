NAME
     clion – launch JetBrains CLion Integrated Development Environment

SYNOPSIS
     clion [jvm-args]

DESCRIPTION
     clion starts up the JetBrains CLion Integrated Development Environment.

     The web site https://www.jetbrains.com/clion/ .

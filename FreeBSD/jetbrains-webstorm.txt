NAME
     webstorm – launch JetBrains WebStorm Integrated Development Environment

SYNOPSIS
     webstorm [jvm-args]

DESCRIPTION
     webstorm starts up the JetBrains WebStorm Integrated Development
     Environment.

SEE ALSO
     The web site https://www.jetbrains.com/webstorm/ .

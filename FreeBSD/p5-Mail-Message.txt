NAME
     Mail::Box::FastScalar - fast alternative to IO::Scalar

SYNOPSIS
       my $fh = Mail::Box::FastScalar->new;
       $fh->open(\my $out);

       my $fh = Mail::Box::FastScalar->new(\my $out);

DESCRIPTION
     Extremely fast IO::Scalar replacement - over 20x improvement in getline()
     and getlines() methods.

     Contributed by "Todd Richmond" ("richmond@proofpoint.com")

   Warnings
     You cannot modify the original reference between calls unless you
     "$obj-"seek(1, 0)> to reset the object - VERY rare usage case.

     $/ must be "undef" or string - "" and \scalar unimplemented

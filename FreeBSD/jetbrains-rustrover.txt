NAME
     rustrover – launch JetBrains RustRover Integrated Development Environment

SYNOPSIS
     rustrover [jvm-args]

DESCRIPTION
     rustrover starts up the JetBrains RustRover Integrated Development
     Environment.

SEE ALSO
     The web site https://www.jetbrains.com/rust/ .

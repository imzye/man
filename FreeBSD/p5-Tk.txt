NAME
     ptked - an editor in Perl/Tk

SYNOPSIS
       ptked [-server] [-encoding encoding] [-geometry geometry]
     [file-to-edit]

DESCRIPTION
     ptked is a simple text editor based on perl/Tk's TextUndo widget.

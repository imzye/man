NAME
     pacman.c - Simple Pacman clone

SYNOPSIS
     pacman.c

DESCRIPTION
     Pacman is a yellow, circular character that you move through a maze
     (arrow keys), eating small dots while avoiding colorful ghosts.  When he
     eats special Power dots, he can temporarily turn the tables and eat the
     ghosts for extra points.

     You can switch between fullscreen and windowed mode using the f key.

AUTHORS

NAME
       Dosage - Keep track of your treatments

SYNOPSIS
       io.github.diegopvlk.Dosage [OPTION…]

DESCRIPTION
       Easily manage and track treatments with Dosage: notifications, history, multiple doses, flexible frequency, customization, stock monitoring, and dura‐
       tion control.

OPTIONS
       -h, --help - Show help options

       --help-all - Show all help options

       --help-gapplication - Show GApplication options

       --gapplication-service - Enter GApplication service mode (use from D-Bus service files)

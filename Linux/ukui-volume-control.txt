NAME
       ukui-audio-service - UKUI audio service (tray/control center backend)

SYNOPSIS
       ukui-audio-service [options]

DESCRIPTION
       ukui-audio-service is the background service used by UKUI to provide audio device and volume management for the control center and related components.

OPTIONS
       This program currently does not document any stable command line options.

FILES
       /usr/share/glib-2.0/schemas
              GSettings schemas used by the service.

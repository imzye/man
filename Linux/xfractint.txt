NAME
       xtfractint - display xfractint in an terminal

SYNOPSIS
       xtfractint [ xfracint options ]

DESCRIPTION
       The  xtfractint  opens  an xterm(1) or a rxvt(1) and calls xfractint(1) inside.  This program is nothing more than a wrapper to xfractint to provide a
       simple interface for including it into menues of your favourite window manager.  You may call it with arguments which will be passed to xfracint.

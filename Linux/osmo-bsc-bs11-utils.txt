NAME
       isdnsync - use a cheap HFC-S card with the public telephone network as frequency standard for your E1 line

SYNOPSIS
       isdnsync <card>

DESCRIPTION
       isdnsync  is  a  small tool that initializes a cheap HFC-S ISDN-Card so that bit and frameclock are present on 2 IC-Pins. These signals can be used to
       feed HFC-E1 card with a very exact clock.

SEE ALSO
       osmo-bsc(1)

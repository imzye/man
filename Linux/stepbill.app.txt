NAME
       StepBill - classic computer game

SYNOPSIS
       StepBill

DESCRIPTION
       This  manual  page documents briefly the StepBill command.  This manual page was written for the Debian distribution because the original program does
       not have a manual page.

       Yet again, the fate of the world rests in your hands! An evil computer hacker, known only by his handle 'Bill',  has  created  the  ultimate  computer
       virus.  A virus so powerful that it has the power to transmute an ordinary computer into a toaster oven. (oooh!) 'Bill' has cloned himself into a bil‐
       lion-jillion micro-Bills. Their sole purpose is to deliver the nefarious virus, which has been cleverly disguised as a popular operating  system.   As
       System Administrator/Exterminator, your job is to keep Bill from succeeding at his task.

SEE ALSO
       GNUstep(7).

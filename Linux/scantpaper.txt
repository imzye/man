            [--import IMPORT_FILES [IMPORT_FILES ...]]  [--import-all IMPORT_ALL [IMPORT_ALL ...]] [--locale LOCALE] [--log LOG] [--version] [--debug] [--info] [--warn] [--error] [--fatal]

     A GUI to produce PDFs or DjVus from scanned documents

     --info

     --warn

NAME
       innduct-stats-report - summarise transfer statistics logged by innduct

SYNOPSIS
       innduct-stats-report [logfile ...]

DESCRIPTION
       innduct-stats-report reads logfiles mentioned on its command line, or a single logfile stream fed on stdin, and produces a summary listing of articles
       logged by innnduct instances, one line per site.

BUGS
       How to deal with long site names should be configurable.  In general more flexibility and functionality would be good.

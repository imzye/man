NAME
     plus42bin - HP-42S-inspired calculator

SYNOPSIS
     plus42bin [-compactmenu] [-skin [<skin>]]

DESCRIPTION
     Plus42 is an advanced scientific programmable calculator, based on Free42, a re-implementation of the HP-42S scientific programmable RPN calculator.

     Plus42 adds a larger display, algebraic expressions modeled on those of the HP-27S and HP-17B/19B, units and conversions modeled on those of the HP-48/49/50 series, directories, TVM and amortization
     tables, and function plotting.

OPTIONS
     -compactmenu
            Enables the compact menu.

     -skin [skin]
            Chooses  a skin. Skins must be placed in one of /usr/share/free42, /usr/share/free42/skins, /usr/local/share/free42, /usr/local/share/free42/skins, $XDG_DATA_HOME/free42, or if $XDG_DATA_HOME
            isn't defined, ~/.local/share/free42.

NAME
       elixir-compose - reformat 'elixir compose' output

DESCRIPTION
       elixir-compose  can  be  used for reformatting the general output of elixir compose and produces the contents of lib/ElixirFM/Data/Compose.PL which is
       used in lib/ElixirFM/Resolve.pm.

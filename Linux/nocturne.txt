NAME
     nocturne - Adwaita Music Player / Library Manager

SYNOPSIS
     nocturne

DESCRIPTION
     Nocturne  is  a  Navidrome  /  Jellyfin client that brings all your music together in one place, Nocturne not only connects to existing instances but it's capable of installing and managing it's own
     Navidrome instance.

NAME
       ktowers - X window towers solitaire

SYNOPSIS
       ktowers

DESCRIPTION
       Ktowers  brings  up a window for a towers solatiaire game.  Clicking on a card moves it to the "best" legal play. Dragging a card places it (if legal)
       in the target stack.

RULES
       Tableaus build down in suit order. Single cards may be moved among the tableaus. Empty tableaus may be filled with Kings.  Stocks hold any single card
       each.  Foundations build up in suit from the Ace.

       Goal: move all cards to the foundations.

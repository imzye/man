NAME
       bz3less - view bzip3-compressed files

SYNOPSIS
       bz3less [flags] [file ...]

DESCRIPTION
       bz3less runs less(1) on files or stdin, if no files argument is given, after decompressing them with bz3cat(1).

SEE ALSO
       bzip3(1), less(1)

NAME
       srp_daemon.service - srp_daemon systemd service that controls all ports

SYNOPSIS
       srp_daemon.service

DESCRIPTION
       The srp_daemon.service controls whether or not any srp_daemon processes are running. Although no srp_daemon processes are controlled directly by the
       srp_daemon.service, this service controls whether or not any srp_daemon_port@.service are allowed to be active. Each srp_daemon_port@.service controls
       one srp_daemon process.

SEE ALSO
       srp_daemon(1), srp_daemon_port@.service(5), systemctl(1)

NAME
       postal1 - POSTAL 1

SYNOPSIS
       postal1

DESCRIPTION
       postal1 is an isometric top-down shooter video game originally published in 1997.

       The  player  assume  the role of the Postal Dude, a man who commits mass murder throughout the fictional town of Paradise, Arizona to cure what he be‐
       lieves to be a "hate plague" released by the United States Air Force.

OPTIONS
       No option can be passed to the game on the command line.

FILES
       /usr/share/games/postal1/
              Game data files.

       $XDG_DATA_HOME/PostalPlus/
              defaulting to ~/.local/share/PostalPlus/

              Configuration and state for POSTAL 1.

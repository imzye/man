NAME
       adtrack2 - userfriendly tracker aimed for the OPL3 FM-chip

SYNOPSIS
       adtrack2 [options] files...

DESCRIPTION
       This manual page documents briefly the adtrack2 command.

       adtrack2  is,  dare we say, the most userfriendly tracker aimed for the OPL3 FM-chip, and is full of advanced features to simplify your task of making
       the most of your Adlib-tunes. Supporting 4 operator instruments, percussion mode, a wide selection of importable song-  and  instrument  types  and  -
       maybe foremost - an advanced macro editor that can really push the FM-chip to the limit.

   Supported file formats
       A2M    AdlibTracker2

       A2P    AdlibTracker2 [pattern]

       A2T    AdlibTracker2 [tiny module]

       AMD    Amusic

       CFF    BoomTracker 4.0

       DFM    Digital-FM

       FMK    FM-Kingtracker

       HSC    HSC AdLib Composer / HSC-Tracker

       MTK    MPU-401 trakker

       RAD    Reality AdLib Tracker

       S3M    Scream Tracker 3.x

       SAT    Surprise! AdLib Tracker {ver.1,5,6}

       SA2    Surprise! AdLib Tracker 2.0 {ver.8,9}

       XMS    XMS-Tracker

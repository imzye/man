NAME
       gnb_crypto - generate private_key_file and public_key_file

DESCRIPTION
       usage: gnb_crypto -c -p private_key_file -k public_key_file

Example:
       gnb_crypto -c -p 1001.private -k 1001.public

       gnb(8), gnb_ctl(8), gnb_es(8)

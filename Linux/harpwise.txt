NAME
       harpwise - A harmonica tool for the command line, using microphone and speaker.

SYNOPSIS
       harpwise {MODE} {ARGUMENTS} [OPTIONS]

DESCRIPTION
       harpwise  supports your harmonica practice with scales, chords and licks.  It offers various tools, an explorable quiz and may guide your jamming.  It
       can be used with diatonic (richter) or chromatic harmonicas in different keys.

       Invoke without arguments for introduction and usage:

              harpwise

       To read more details, usage examples and options for the individual modes, invoke them without arguments, e.g.

              harpwise listen

       See

       https://marcihm.github.io/harpwise

       for full documentation.

EXAMPLES
       See the usage information of the individual modes.

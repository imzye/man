NAME
     fortunes‐dhammapada

SYNOPSIS

     fortune [TRANSLATION]

     Displays a verse from the Dhammapada using fortune

DESCRIPTION

     The Dhammapada is an anthology of 423 verses from the Kuddaka Nikaya part of the Theravada Pali Canon.  It contains Buddhist ethical and spiritual precepts.

      Translations  available: 2 English translations (by John Richard or F. Max Muller),
      German, or Polish.

     NOTE: Verses may contain 1 or more numbers or numbered paragraphs

TRANSLATIONS

     dhammapada English - John Richard's Translation.

     dhammapada.m English - F. Max Muller's Translation.

     dhammapada.de German Translation.

     dhammapada.pl Polish Translation.

EXAMPLES

     fortune dhammapada : Displays a random verse from Richard's translation.

     fortune dhammapada.m : Displays a random verse from Muller's translation.

     fortune dhammapada.de : Displays a random verse from German translation.

     fortune dhammapada.pl : Displays a random verse from Polish translation.

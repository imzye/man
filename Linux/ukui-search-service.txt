NAME
       ukui-search-service-dir-manager - service to manage all the directories' mount info

SYNOPSIS
       ukui-search-service-dir-manager [options]

OPTIONS
       -h, --help
              Displays help on commandline options.

       --help-all
              Displays help, including generic Qt options.

       -v, --version
              Displays version information.

       -q, --quit
              Quit DBus service

SEE ALSO
       The  full  documentation  for ukui-search-service-dir-manager is maintained as a Texinfo manual.  If the info and ukui-search-service-dir-manager pro‐
       grams are properly installed at your site, the command

              info ukui-search-service-dir-manager

       should give you access to the complete manual.

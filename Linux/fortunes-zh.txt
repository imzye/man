NAME
       fortune-zh - Chinese Data files for fortune

SYNOPSIS
       fortune-zh

DESCRIPTION
       fortune-zh is a simple shell script that invokes fortune with Chinese data files and their preset proportions.

DATAFILE
       /usr/share/games/fortunes/tang300
              About 300 Poetries from Tang dynasty.

       /usr/share/games/fortunes/song100
              About 100 Poetries from Song dynasty.

       /usr/share/games/fortunes/chinese
              Miscellaneous Cookies in Chinese language, both ancient and modern, excluding those tang300 and song100 already included.
